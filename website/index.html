Features

Built for QA, made simple

From one-liners to full test suites — QA Genie adapts to your workflow.

🤖

AI-Powered Generation

Describe what you want to test in plain English. QA Genie generates structured, actionable test cases in seconds — ready for review.

⚡

Offline Fallback

When the generation service is unavailable, a built-in fallback engine produces test cases so you're never left empty-handed.

📄

Export Anywhere

Export test cases to Excel, CSV, JSON, or PDF. Share with your team, import into test management tools, or keep them organized in suites.

🗄

Smart Suites

Organize, edit, and manage test suites locally. Mark status, reorder cases, and track progress — all synced securely when you're online.

⚠

Risk-Based Sorting

Sort test cases by risk level automatically — security and high-priority cases rise to the top for focused testing.

🗒

Batch Operations

Select multiple test cases at once to copy, move, or delete — streamlining suite management.

How It Works

Three simple steps

No setup, no configuration. Just describe and generate.

Describe

Tell QA Genie what you're testing — a login flow, a payment gateway, an API endpoint. Natural language works.

2

Generate

Describe what to test, pick a platform, and QA Genie generates a structured test suite with functional, edge case, and error scenarios — tailored to your platform.

3

Export & Ship

Review, edit, and export. Share with your team or import into your test management workflow.
