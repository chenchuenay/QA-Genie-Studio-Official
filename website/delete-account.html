Delete Account

Last updated: July 4, 2026

To request deletion of your QA Genie Studio account and all associated data, send an email to wipe@qagenies.com.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

Open the app and go to Account → Delete Account. Your authentication credentials, usage data, and synced test cases are removed instantly. Your profile identifier is kept for 24 hours to prevent immediate re-registration (fraud prevention), then automatically deleted. No take backs.

Method 2: Web Request (Email)

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to wipe@qagenies.com from your registered email address.

Requirements

  • You must send the email from the same email address registered in my database — I verify your identity against your registered email.
  • Include your account details in the email (the Google email address you signed up with, and optionally the reason for deletion).

Processing Time

After I receive and verify your request, your account will be completely deleted within 24–48 hours. All your data — profile, synced test cases, usage history — will be permanently removed.

⚠️ No take backs. Once deletion is processed, it is irreversible. There is no undo. All your data will be permanently erased from my systems.
