Why QA Genie Studio

In a world of free AI

Free AI chatbots are powerful, but they're not built for QA workflows. Here's what makes QA Genie Studio different.

  • Structured output, not raw text

    Free AI generates paragraphs. QA Genie Studio produces structured test cases with preconditions, steps, expected results, test data, priority, and status — ready for review.

  • Export to real QA formats

    Free AI output stays in the chat. QA Genie Studio exports to Excel (.xlsx), CSV, JSON (Xray-compatible), and PDF — formats that work in Jira, test management tools, and team handoffs.

  • Test summary reports

    Generate a concise summary report with pass/fail rates, priority breakdowns, and full test results — exportable as PDF for team handoffs, sprint reviews, or QA sign-off.

  • Suite management — save, revisit, edit

    Free AI is ephemeral. QA Genie Studio saves test suites locally, lets you revisit, edit cases, mark status (pass/fail/blocked), and reorder — all without losing progress.

  • Cloud sync — access from any device

    Sign in with Google and your test suites follow you. Generate on your phone, review on a tablet, export from anywhere. Guest data stays on-device by default.

  • Reliable fallback engine

    When AI is unavailable or returns bad output, QA Genie Studio's local fallback still delivers quality test cases. No blank screens, no "try again later."

  • Privacy-first by design

    Guest data stays entirely on your device. No training on your test cases. No account required to try. Your QA work is yours.
