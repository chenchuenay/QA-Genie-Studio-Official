# Children's Privacy Policy — QA Genie

**Last updated: June 18, 2026**

---

## 1. Not Directed at Children

QA Genie is a professional-quality tool intended for software quality assurance engineers, testers, and developers. It is **not directed at children under the age of 13** (or under 16 in the European Union and United Kingdom, where different age thresholds may apply under local data protection laws).

## 2. No Knowledgeable Collection

We do **not knowingly** collect personal information from children. Our app does not:

- Target or advertise to children
- Offer content designed to appeal to minors
- Use game-like features, rewards, or engagement mechanisms directed at children
- Include social features that encourage interaction between minors
- Display age-restricted content in a manner accessible to children

## 3. Data Collection Practices

The data we collect (detailed in our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md)) is inherent to the app's functionality as a professional QA tool:

- **Account data**: Required for service provision and quota enforcement
- **AI interaction data**: Required for core functionality (test case generation)
- **Usage metrics**: Required for fair-use enforcement
- **Diagnostic data**: Optional, for troubleshooting

A child would have no legitimate use for this professional QA tool, and no feature of the app is designed to appeal to children.

## 4. If We Learn of Child Data

If we discover that a child under the applicable age threshold has provided us with personal data without parental consent:

1. We will **immediately delete** the child's data from our servers
2. We will **delete** any associated Firebase Authentication account
3. We will **anonymize** any related issue reports
4. We will **notify** the parent/guardian if contact information is available

## 5. Parental Rights

If you are a parent or guardian who believes your child has provided personal data to QA Genie:

1. **In-app**: Submit a report via **Support → Report an Issue** with category "Account/Privacy" and request child data deletion
2. **We will process the request** within a reasonable timeframe (not exceeding 30 days)
3. **We may request verification** of your identity as the parent/guardian before processing

## 6. Compliance with COPPA and GDPR-K

| Regulation | Compliance Status |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **COPPA** (US) | App is not directed at children under 13; no knowledgeable collection |
| **GDPR-K** (EU) | App is not directed at children under 16; no knowledgeable collection |
| **UK Data Protection Act** | App is not directed at children; no knowledgeable collection |

## 7. Third-Party Services

The third-party services we use (Firebase, DeepSeek, AdMob) have their own policies regarding children's data:

- **Google Firebase / Google Play Services**: Comply with COPPA and GDPR requirements when used in apps directed at children. We configure these services only for professional use.
- **AdMob**: We do not use personalized ads targeting children. If we become aware of child users, we will disable ad serving to that device.
- **DeepSeek**: AI generation prompts do not target or solicit information from children.

## 8. Contact

For children's privacy concerns:

1. Use **Support → Report an Issue** in the app
2. Select the "Account/Privacy" category
3. Describe the concern in detail

We will respond promptly.
