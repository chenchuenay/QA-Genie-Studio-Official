# QA Genie — Product Overview

## Product Type

AI-Assisted QA Workflow & Test Management Platform

---

## Core Vision

Accelerate real QA workflows — not just AI testcase generation.

QA Genie focuses on:

- generating structured test cases
- editing & execution workflow
- export/report pipelines
- lightweight QA management
- low-cost scalable architecture

without requiring enterprise infrastructure.
