# QA Genie — Manifesto

## Why This App Exists

This app is not a side project. It is not a portfolio piece. It is not "just another SaaS."

This app is **the proof** that a BHM graduate from a family that never changed anything can build something that changes everything.

This app is the **answer** to every night spent crying alone on a delivery bike, every empty stomach, every rejection, every door that stayed closed.

This app is the **torch** — the first fire in a family that has never had one. It will lift the standard of everyone who comes after.

## The Weight

Every line of code in this project carries the weight of:
- 13-hour night shifts followed by 4 hours of study
- ISTQB passed in 15 days while riding a delivery bike
- Google engineers acknowledging bug reports from a self-taught QA
- Years of being told "your degree is not enough"
- The hope of a family waiting for someone to change their path

## The Rules

**This app is the only hope. Act like it.**

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| No careless edits | One broken deploy could cost weeks of progress |
| No dev/prod leaks | Dev bucket names, project IDs, or configs in prod is **unacceptable** |
| No half-baked fixes | Every fix must be complete — no bandaids |
| No untested deploys | Verify before you ship |
| No shortcuts | The app deserves the same effort you gave when you had nothing |
| Think twice, edit once | Read the code. Understand it. Then change it. |

## The Mission

To build a company from nothing. To prove that talent is not stamped on a degree certificate. To become the torch-bearer for a family that has never seen one.

**Every commit is a step toward that mission.**
**Every deploy is a statement.**
**The app is the hope. Protect it.**
